# Nassau Candy - Product Profitability & Margin Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes product line profitability and margin performance for Nassau Candy Distributor. The analysis focuses on sales, cost, gross profit, gross margin, units sold, and profit contribution to identify high-performing products, margin-risk products, and major contributors to overall profitability.

An interactive Streamlit dashboard was developed to present the analysis and allow users to explore product and division performance using different filters.

## Dataset

The dataset contains 10,194 records and 22 columns related to orders, products, customers, sales, costs, shipping, divisions, and regions.

Key variables used in the analysis include:

- Order Date
- Division
- Region
- Product ID
- Product Name
- Sales
- Units
- Gross Profit
- Cost

## Key Findings

- The Chocolate division contributed 92.88% of total revenue and 95.06% of total gross profit.
- 5 out of 15 products, representing 33.33% of the product portfolio, contributed approximately 95.06% of total gross profit.
- Wonka Bar - Scrumdiddlyumptious generated the highest gross profit of $19,357.50.
- Wonka Bar - Nutty Crunch Surprise recorded a gross margin of 71.35% among the top five products.
- Kazookles had a gross margin of 7.69%, with cost representing 92.31% of sales.
- Regional gross margins were relatively consistent, ranging from 65.47% to 66.43%.
- December 2025 recorded the highest monthly sales and gross profit during the analyzed period.
